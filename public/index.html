Navi Position-based budgeting, budget playground, investment optimization, and payroll integration.
Mantis AI intelligence hub with natural language analysis, grant discovery, anomaly detection, and compliance checking.
Vatica Automated reporting, general ledger drill-down, regulatory data, and spreadsheet export.
